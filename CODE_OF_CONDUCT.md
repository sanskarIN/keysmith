# Code of Conduct

KeySmith follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles: be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and focused on a harassment-free community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban contributions or participation that violate these expectations.

Report community conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Security vulnerabilities must instead follow `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and apply proportionate, consistent responses.
